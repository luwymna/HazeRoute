Health & safety reminders
Always check the official IPU first: apims.doe.gov.my — during haze season this panel may underestimate risk.

What API/IPU levels mean (official Malaysian scale):

  • 0–50 (Good): Safe for all outdoor activities.
  • 51–100 (Moderate): Sensitive groups should watch for symptoms.
  • 101–200 (Unhealthy): Limit outdoor exertion. Wear an N95 mask if outside. Close windows.
  • 201–300 (Very Unhealthy): Stay indoors. Avoid outdoor exercise. Use an air purifier if available.
  • 301+ (Hazardous): Emergency conditions. Remain indoors with windows sealed. Seek medical help for breathing difficulty.

General precautions:

  • Drink water frequently — helps your body cope with airborne particles.
  • Keep windows and doors closed when API is above 100.
  • Wear a fitted N95 or KN95 mask outdoors (surgical masks don't filter PM2.5).
  • Avoid outdoor exercise when API is above 100; move workouts indoors.
  • Use an air purifier with a HEPA filter indoors if you have one.
  • Children, the elderly, pregnant women, and those with asthma or heart conditions are at higher risk — take extra care.
  • Seek medical help if you experience chest pain, severe shortness of breath, or persistent coughing.

Health thresholds follow the Department of Environment (DOE) Malaysia IPU scale. Official readings and advisories: apims.doe.gov.my
